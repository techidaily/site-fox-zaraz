## Avoid These Song Choices for a Memorable Wedding DJ Set

[![Songs not to play at weddings](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/dontplaysongs-coverimage-1.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/dontplaysongs-coverimage-1.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Songs not to play at weddings")

Digital Music News recently published an article “[Brides are Telling Wedding DJs Not to Play These Songs](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2017/08/09/wedding-djs-banned-songs/)” and it seems it’s finally time to put the “Chicken Dance” to bed.

According to the report that’s not the only mainstay song that should get the ol’ “shift + delete”. Quite a few wedding DJs will need to re-arrange their wedding playlists after viewing this list!

> #### _“Hickey surveyed 182 wedding playlists submitted between May 31st and June 10th. He collected the testimonies of over two dozen professional wedding DJs on nearly 200 weddings.”_

In my personal opinion this is welcomed news, albeit a couple years late — If I have to play the Cupid Shuffle of do the Hokey Pokey one more time I may melt my laptop.

Of course, there are a few tracks on this list that’ll have to stay put. As much as it’s cliche, “You Shook Me All Night Long” will always have a home in my library.
