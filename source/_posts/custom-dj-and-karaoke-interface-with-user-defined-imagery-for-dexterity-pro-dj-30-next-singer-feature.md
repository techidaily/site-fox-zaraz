## Custom DJ & Karaoke Interface with User-Defined Imagery for Dexterity Pro DJ 3.0 Next Singer Feature

[![use background image or video behind karaoke screen](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/nextsingerdisplayimage-template.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/nextsingerdisplayimage-template.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "use background image or video behind karaoke screen")

**New in DEX 3.7 (public beta at time of post) is the ability to use your own background image or video behind the karaoke ‘Next Singers’ display.**

[DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) isn’t just a **DJ program for mixing music and music videos, you can also host karaoke** with the included selection of karaoke-specific features such a key control (key stepper), singers list, lyrics-only output and ‘next singers’ display.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![DEX 3.7 Next Singers Display For Karaoke](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/nextsingerdisplay-screenshot.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "DEX 3.7 Next Singers Display For Karaoke")](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/nextsingerdisplay-screenshot.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1)

DEX 3’s ‘next singers’ display showcases your next 3 singers on the singers screen (display 2) so **your singers always know who’s up to sing next**. In all previous iterations of [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) you had a basic black background behind your singers. It got the job done, singers knew who was up next — but it wasn’t aesthetically pleasing. It was plain-Jane boring.

Thankfully, **in version 3.7 you can upload and use your own background image or video**. Under the ‘video tab’ in DEX 3’s options you’ll find a new area for ‘**Karaoke Singers List**‘ – it’s here that you **browse and select your image or video file** to display behind the ‘next singers’. If you use a video clip [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) will **automatically (and continuously) loop the video clip** behind the ‘next singers’ display.

The new image and video background feature provides easy method to **create a better looking screen 2** for the audience and your karaoke singers.
