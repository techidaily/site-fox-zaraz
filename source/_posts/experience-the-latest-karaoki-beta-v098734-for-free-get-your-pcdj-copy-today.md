* **Karaoke Emporium Integration:** We’ve added the “Karaoke Emporium” Storefront app right into Karaoki. This isn’t just an add-on; it’s a standalone application that seamlessly communicates with Karaoki when it’s running. Access all the new in-app store options there, including…
* **PartyTyme.net Store:** The PartyTyme.net store is now available in the Karaoke Emporium, expanding your song choices even further. Purchase on-the-fly from a selection of over 34,000 karaoke songs.

#### Changes:

* **Select-A-Track Store Moves:** We’ve moved the Select-A-Track store to the Karaoke Emporium.
* **Streamlining Karaoki:** To make things more efficient, we’ve removed the Select-A-Track store directly from Karaoki.

* **Edit Tracks Glitch Fixed:** We’ve squashed a pesky bug in the “Edit Tracks” feature where the Previous/Next Buttons weren’t updating the Genre field correctly.
