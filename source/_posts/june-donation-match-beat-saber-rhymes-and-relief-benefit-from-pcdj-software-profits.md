## June Donation Match: Beat Saber, Rhymes & Relief Benefit From PCDJ Software Profits

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/restorehappy-blog1.jpg?resize=564%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/restorehappy-blog1.jpg?fit=564%2C306&ssl=1 "restorehappy-blog")

**This June, your PCDJ Software purchase _can make a difference._** 

**DJ software** is what we _‘do’,_ and it is a lot of fun (_probably more than it should be!_), but here at [**PCDJ**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) we strive to give back to the community whenever an opportunity we believe in presents itself. Never before has a charitable organization been more in-tune with what we’re about than **[Beats Rhymes and Relief](http://beatsrhymesandrelief.org/)** – the perfect mix of music and art with a steadfast mission to raise awareness of human and social issues.

**Beats, Rhymes & Relief** is a non-profit organization utilizing the arts to raise awareness and support for worldwide humanitarian relief efforts. They[![gI_69530_1506665_708763166110_4574629785409607885_n](https://pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/gI_69530_1506665_708763166110_4574629785409607885_n.png)](https://pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/gI%5F69530%5F1506665%5F708763166110%5F4574629785409607885%5Fn.png) harness the power of cultural diplomacy to raise the profile of global issues through high-profile public music concerts. 

The non-profits current mission is to [**#RestoreHappy though a public awareness video aimed at raising awareness and humanizing the plight of Syria’s children caught in 3 years of conflict.**](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) The footage in the video captures diverse children dancing to Pharrell Williams “Happy” which was blaring on portable speakers hanging around the neck of the filmmaker. 

**For all of this June, PCDJ will proudly donate 10% of all [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) net proceeds to Beats, Rhymes and Relief.**
