## Karaoke Tool Tips: Masterful Techniques for Adding Songs for Artists in PCDJ Software

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/karaoketips-coverimagejune.jpg?resize=530%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/06/karaoketips-coverimagejune.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "karaoketips-coverimagejune")

PCDJ Karaoki is our [best karaoke software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for professional karaoke hosts. While our [DEX DJ Software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) includes a basic singers list and key control, in addition to DJ and video mixing capabilities, when it comes to hosting karaoke shows with in-depth singer and singer history management _nothing_ beats Karaoki.

In this addition of Karaoke Tips we show you two ways you can enter in your singers – one using the singer date screen (where you can also include contact info and comments for a singer), and the other just using the drag-and-drop method to the singer rotation list area. Both methods accomplish the same goal, but if you want to set up singers pre-gig than using the singer date screen is the way to go. We also briefly demonstrate how the automatic singer rotation list works. Jennifer Lopez even makes a cameo! 😉
