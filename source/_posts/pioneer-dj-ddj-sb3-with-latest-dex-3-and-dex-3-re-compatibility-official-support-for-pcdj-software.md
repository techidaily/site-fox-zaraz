The Pioneer DJ **DDJ-SB3** controller is now DEX 3 and DEX 3 RE **DJ software** supported (Download the MAP below).

The **DDJ-SB3** improves on the renowned, and last-years top seller among _all_ [DJ controllers](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), DDJ-SB2, which was embraced by a wide range of DJs – from beginners, to mobile DJs, to club jocks – thanks to its combination of professional features and easy portability. Now that our DJ controller mapping guru’s have created a MAP [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJs can enjoy 2-deck mixing with the **DDJ-SB3**.

[VIEW LARGER IMAGE ![Pioneer DJ DDJ-SB3 DJ Controller Top](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/ddj-sb3-main.png?fit=300%2C225&ssl=1 "Pioneer DJ DDJ-SB3 DJ Controller Top")](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/ddj-sb3-main.png?fit=800%2C600&ssl=1)

Pioneer DJ has improved the interface of the **DDJ-SB3**, making the layout even more intuitive to use than its predecessors and by inheriting traits from the layouts of the top models in the DDJ-S series — including the introduction of the dedicated play and cue buttons, and an independent ‘Auto Loop’ button.

Priced at only $249 on most online DJ equipment retailer websites, the **DDJ-SB3** is sure to be a hit just like it’s predecessor was for [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) and [DEX 3 RE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) users.
