## S Button Discovery: Mastering the DEX

We’ve added a swifter method for searching karaoke singer history in DEX 3.14 (and up).

To locate singers and their personal singer history in prior versions of [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) you were required to type in an S and then a colon (:) and the singers name immediately thereafter. To speed up the process, we’ve added a new “S” button to the search box window.

When enabled (clicked) the button will turn red in color to indicate singer search is on. Type in the name of the karaoke singer you’re attempting to locate and their history will display immediately in the search results (along with key changes, when applicable).

We hope the addition of the “S” button and singer search helps make your karaoke events that much easier to navigate!
